E-Filing Portal

Schedule-by-Schedule Portal Navigation

Follow TaxSarthi's exact sequence on incometax.gov.in

Enter Gross Salary under section 17(1), Perquisites under section 17(2), and Profits in lieu of salary under section 17(3). The statutory Standard Deduction of ₹75,000 (New Regime) or ₹50,000 (Old Regime) is deducted automatically. Professional tax is claimed under section 16(iii).
Enter Equity STCG under Section 111A (taxed at 20%) and Equity LTCG under Section 112A (taxed at 12.5% on gains exceeding the ₹1,25,000 exemption). TaxSarthi cross-checks your aggregate broker Tax P&L against AIS SFT codes.
Declare Savings bank interest, Fixed Deposit interest, Dividend receipts, and VDA/crypto earnings. VDA gains are reported in Schedule VDA and taxed at a flat 30% without deduction or loss set-off.
Reconcile the portal's computed final tax liability against TaxSarthi's calculation pack. The final three actions belong strictly to you: Pay, Submit, and e-Verify.
